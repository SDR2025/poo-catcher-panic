# Poo Catcher Panic

A silly 8-lane cartoon rhythm-catching game. Slide between toilets, clap to catch the falling poop, chain combos, and survive the escalating chaos.

**[Play it here](https://SDR2025.github.io/poo-catcher-panic/)**

Best played in landscape - on a phone, just rotate your screen when prompted.

Controls: A/D or arrow keys to move, Space to clap (or the on-screen buttons on a touchscreen).
